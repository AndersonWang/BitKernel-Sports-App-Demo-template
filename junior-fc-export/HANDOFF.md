Framework v1.1.0 · updated 6 Nov 2026 · team package for **Junior FC (Junior de Barranquilla)** · owner Anderson Wang, BitKernel Design

## 3. How to update it

**Content (no code):** Configure, see CONFIGURATION.md. Changes stay in your browser; send them with Export config or Download offline copy.

**Built-in content (AI agent or developer, needs the source package):**

| To change | Where in the source |
|---|---|
| Junior photos and videos | `public/assets/themes/junior-de-barranquilla-light/<same path as the shipped file>`, listed in `src/tokens/theme-assets.json` |
| Club text and find/replace rules | `src/tokens/theme-text.json` (theme pack) and `src/editions/junior/theme-text.json` (Junior-only copy) |
| Squad, league table, opening colours | `src/tokens/theme-squads.json`, `theme-tables.json`, `theme-launch.json` |
| Colours | `src/tokens/semantics.json`, mode "Junior de Barranquilla (Light)" |
| This guide | `src/editions/junior/HANDOFF.md` (README and CONFIGURATION are generated by `scripts/docs.mjs`) |

Then `npm run build:junior`, check the changed screens, publish `dist-junior/` to the online link (more than 255 files: two publishes), make the offline zip, and add a line to the change log below.

## 4. Open questions for the club

- **Venue:** the demo says Estadio Metropolitano (Barranquilla). 2026 reports say Junior plays home games in Cartagena during the renovation. Confirm.
- **League table:** points and goal difference are placeholder demo numbers.
- **Trophies:** the Team page "Last won" years are placeholders (e.g. Copa Libertadores "Last won 2021": Junior hasn't won it). Replace with the club's real honours.
- **Opponents:** opponents 1 and 4 are both Millonarios (from the theme pack). Pick a different fourth opponent.
- **Copy to confirm:** fan name, slogan, channel and show names, fan clubs, voting campaign name, derby rival, B team name.
- **Photo rights:** photos were supplied for demo use. Check usage rights before any showing outside a pitch.
- **Voting banner:** the kits in the campaign banner look like Atlético de Madrid's. Replace with a Junior image.
- **Watch chat avatar** (`watch/av-2.webp`) was uploaded as a 720×1280 portrait; a square crop would look cleaner.
- **Figma:** the Junior colour mode isn't in Figma yet (by decision; Anderson adds it by hand). Until then a token sync from Figma would drop it.
